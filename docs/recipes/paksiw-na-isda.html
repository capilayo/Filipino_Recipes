Paksiw na Isda

Fish simmered in vinegar, garlic, ginger, and spices — simple, tangy, and the perfect everyday ulam.

?? Main Dish? 10 min prep?? 20 min cook?? 3–4 servings? Easy

?? Description

Paksiw na Isda is a simple and classic Filipino dish where fish is simmered in vinegar, garlic, ginger, and spices until tender and tangy. It's one of the quickest Filipino dishes to prepare — minimal ingredients, maximum flavor.

?? Ingredients

  • 500g bangus (milkfish), tilapia, or any whole fish, cleaned and sliced
  • ½ cup white vinegar (or sukang paombong)
  • ½ cup water
  • 5 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger, sliced
  • 1 pc long green chili (siling haba)
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis) or salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 pcs eggplant, sliced (optional)

????? Instructions

  1. Arrange garlic, ginger, and eggplant (if using) at the bottom of a shallow pot.
  2. Place the fish pieces on top.
  3. Pour in vinegar, water, and oil. Add peppercorns and chili. Season with fish sauce.
  4. Bring to a boil over medium heat. Do not stir — let the vinegar cook off.
  5. Reduce heat and simmer for 10–15 minutes until fish is cooked through.
  6. Serve immediately with hot white rice.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Never stir paksiw while cooking — it breaks the fish and makes the sauce cloudy.
  • Sukang Paombong (palm vinegar) gives a more traditional, mellow flavor.
  • Adding eggplant (talong) soaks up the vinegary sauce beautifully.
