Leche Flan

Silky Filipino caramel custard made with egg yolks and condensed milk — the crown jewel of every fiesta dessert table.

?? Dessert? 20 min prep?? 35 min steam?? 8–10 servings? Medium

?? Description

Leche Flan is the crown jewel of Filipino desserts — a rich, silky-smooth caramel custard made with egg yolks and condensed milk, steamed in an oval llanera mold until perfectly set. Found at every fiesta, birthday, and celebration, Leche Flan is the dessert Filipinos are most proud to call their own.

?? Ingredients

Caramel:

  • ¾ cup white sugar

Custard:

  • 10 egg yolks
  • 1 can (300ml) condensed milk
  • 1 can (370ml) evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract · Pinch of salt

????? Instructions

  1. Make caramel: In each llanera, add 3–4 tbsp sugar. Place over medium-low heat. Tilt and swirl gently as sugar melts and turns golden amber. Remove from heat. Let cool until hardened.
  2. Prepare custard: Gently combine egg yolks, condensed milk, evaporated milk, vanilla, and salt. Do NOT beat vigorously — you want no bubbles or foam.
  3. Strain: Pour mixture through a fine sieve to remove any lumps. This gives the silkiest texture.
  4. Gently pour strained custard over hardened caramel in each llanera. Cover tightly with aluminum foil.
  5. Steam: Place molds in a steamer over boiling water. Steam on low-medium heat for 25–35 minutes. Insert a toothpick — it should come out clean when done.
  6. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight is best).
  7. Unmold: Run a thin knife around the edges. Place a plate on top and flip quickly. The caramel will cascade over the top. Serve chilled.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Use egg yolks only (no whites) for the richest, densest flan.
  • Never boil the water during steaming — keep it at a gentle simmer to avoid bubbles in the custard.
  • Wrapping the foil tightly prevents condensation from dripping onto the surface of the flan.
