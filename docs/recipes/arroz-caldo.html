Arroz Caldo

Warm chicken and ginger rice porridge topped with crispy garlic and calamansi � the Filipino comfort food for rainy days.

?? Soup? 10 min prep?? 45 min cook?? 4�6 servings? Easy

?? Description

Arroz Caldo is the Filipino answer to chicken congee � a warm, comforting rice porridge cooked with chicken, ginger, and garlic, topped with crispy fried garlic, spring onions, and a drizzle of calamansi. It's the ultimate Filipino comfort food, perfect for rainy days, ma�anita mornings, or when someone needs healing.

?? Ingredients

  • 500g chicken (thighs or drumsticks), bone-in
  • 1 cup glutinous rice (malagkit) or regular rice
  • 6 cups chicken broth or water
  • 1 thumb ginger, julienned � 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped � 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper � 2 tbsp cooking oil � Salt to taste
  • Toppings: crispy fried garlic, spring onions, hard-boiled eggs, calamansi, fish sauce

????? Instructions

  1. Heat oil. Saut� garlic and ginger until golden and fragrant.
  2. Add onion and cook until translucent. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add fish sauce and stir for 1�2 minutes.
  4. Pour in broth or water. Bring to a boil. Add rice.
  5. Stir regularly to prevent sticking. Simmer on medium-low heat.
  6. Cook for 30�35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rice is very soft and porridge-like.
  7. Season with salt and pepper. Serve hot with toppings.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Use glutinous rice (malagkit) for a thicker, creamier texture.
  • Stir frequently � rice settles and burns at the bottom if left unattended.
  • Topped with tokwa't baboy (fried tofu and pork), it becomes a full meal.
