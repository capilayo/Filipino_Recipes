Monggo Guisado

Hearty mung bean stew sautéed with pork, tomatoes, and malunggay — the classic Filipino Friday dish.

🍲 Soup⏱ 10 min prep🕐 50 min cook👥 6 servings⭐ Easy

📝 Description

Monggo Guisado is a Filipino staple — a hearty mung bean stew sautéed with garlic, onion, tomatoes, and pork, enriched with malunggay or ampalaya leaves. This budget-friendly comfort dish is traditionally served on Fridays and is a beloved feature of the Filipino weekly menu.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 cups dried mung beans (monggo), soaked overnight or 4 hours
  • 200g pork belly, cut into small cubes (or substitute with shrimp/smoked fish)
  • 1 cup malunggay leaves (or ampalaya/bitter melon leaves)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped · 4 cloves garlic, minced · 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis) · 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 4–5 cups water or pork broth · Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp shrimp paste (bagoong alamang), optional

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Drain soaked mung beans. Place in a pot with fresh water. Boil for 30–40 minutes until soft. Set aside.
  2. In a large pan, heat oil over medium heat. Sauté garlic until golden. Add onion and tomatoes; cook until softened.
  3. Add pork cubes and cook until lightly browned. Season with patis.
  4. Pour in the cooked monggo along with its liquid. Add broth if needed. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add malunggay leaves and stir. Simmer 2 more minutes. Adjust seasoning. Serve hot with rice.

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Soaking the mung beans overnight cuts cooking time significantly.
  • Smoked fish (tinapa) makes a delicious swap for pork — very classic combination.
  • Add a dollop of bagoong on top for extra umami punch.
