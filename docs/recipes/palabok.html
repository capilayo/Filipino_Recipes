Pancit Palabok

Rice noodles smothered in vibrant orange shrimp sauce, loaded with chicharon, eggs, and tinapa — a fiesta showstopper.

?? Noodles? 20 min prep?? 30 min cook?? 6–8 servings? Medium

?? Description

Pancit Palabok is a beloved Filipino noodle dish smothered in a vibrant shrimp-based orange sauce, topped with hard-boiled eggs, chicharon, tinapa flakes, spring onions, and calamansi. It's a fiesta favorite and one of the most festive and colorful dishes in Filipino cuisine.

?? Ingredients

Noodles:

  • 400g bihon (rice noodles), soaked in water

Palabok Sauce:

  • 2 cups shrimp broth (from heads and shells)
  • 200g ground pork · 3 tbsp annatto powder (for color)
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour or cornstarch (dissolved)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced · 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis) · Salt and pepper to taste

Toppings:

  • Hard-boiled eggs, sliced · Chicharon (pork crackling), crushed
  • Smoked tinapa flakes · 200g shrimp, boiled and peeled
  • Spring onions, sliced · Calamansi, halved · Fried garlic

????? Instructions

  1. Make shrimp broth: boil shrimp heads and shells in 2–3 cups water for 10 minutes. Strain.
  2. Cook the sauce: sauté garlic, onion, and ground pork. Add broth + annatto water. Simmer.
  3. Add dissolved flour/cornstarch. Stir until sauce thickens. Season with fish sauce.
  4. Cook noodles: boil for 2–3 minutes until just tender. Drain. Arrange on a large platter.
  5. Pour generous sauce over the noodles.
  6. Top with shrimp, egg, chicharon, tinapa, spring onions, and fried garlic.
  7. Serve with calamansi halves on the side.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Annatto (achuete) gives the signature orange color — don't skip it!
  • Soak bihon noodles just until pliable before cooking to avoid mushiness.
  • Add chicharon right before serving so it stays crunchy.
