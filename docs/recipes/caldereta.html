Caldereta

Rich Filipino beef stew in tomato sauce with liver spread, olives, and bell peppers — bold and festive.

?? Main Dish? 15 min prep?? 60 min cook?? 5–6 servings? Medium

?? Description

Caldereta is a rich and robust Filipino stew traditionally made with goat meat, though beef and pork versions are equally popular. Cooked in tomato sauce with liver spread, bell peppers, olives, and potatoes, it's a bold, deeply flavorful dish that's a staple at Filipino celebrations.

?? Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef (or goat / pork), cut into large chunks
  • 1 can (250ml) tomato sauce
  • 3 tbsp liver spread (or mashed chicken liver)
  • 2 medium potatoes, quartered
  • 1 medium carrot, chunked
  • 1 pc red bell pepper, cut into strips
  • 1 pc green bell pepper, cut into strips
  • ½ cup green olives (pitted)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 cup beef broth or water
  • 1–2 pcs bay leaves
  • 1–2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste · 2 tbsp cooking oil

????? Instructions

  1. Heat oil. Sauté garlic and onion until softened.
  2. Add the beef and brown on all sides over medium-high heat.
  3. Pour in tomato sauce, soy sauce, and broth. Add bay leaves. Bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 40–45 minutes until beef is tender.
  5. Add potatoes and carrots. Simmer for 10 minutes until cooked through.
  6. Stir in liver spread, bell peppers, and olives. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  7. Add chili flakes if desired. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Serve hot with steaming white rice.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Liver spread (like Reno brand) is the secret ingredient — deep, rich umami flavor.
  • For extra tender beef, use a pressure cooker for the first 25 minutes.
  • Goat meat is the traditional choice for a more authentic caldereta experience.
