Tortang Talong

Flame-roasted eggplant dipped in egg and pan-fried golden — smoky, simple, and irresistibly satisfying.

?? Main Dish? 15 min prep?? 20 min cook?? 3–4 servings? Easy

?? Description

Tortang Talong is a Filipino eggplant omelette made by roasting talong over an open flame, peeling off the charred skin, dipping it in beaten egg, and pan-frying it to golden perfection. It's simple, smoky, and delicious — one of the most satisfying quick Filipino meals.

?? Ingredients

  • 3–4 pcs medium talong (eggplant), whole
  • 3 pcs eggs, beaten · ½ tsp salt · ¼ tsp ground black pepper · 3 tbsp cooking oil

Optional (giniling version):

  • 200g ground pork or beef · 2 cloves garlic · ½ small onion · 1 tbsp soy sauce

????? Instructions

  1. Roast the eggplant: Place whole eggplants directly over a gas flame. Rotate until skin is completely charred and flesh is soft, about 8–10 minutes.
  2. Let cool slightly. Peel off the charred skin, keeping the stem intact.
  3. Gently flatten each eggplant with a fork to create a fan shape.
  4. Season the beaten eggs with salt and pepper.
  5. Dip the flattened eggplant completely in the egg mixture.
  6. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Slide the egg-coated eggplant into the pan.
  7. Pour a little extra egg over the top. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side until golden.
  8. Serve immediately with rice and banana ketchup or spiced vinegar.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Roasting directly over a flame gives that authentic smoky flavor.
  • The stem helps you handle and flip the eggplant without breaking it.
  • Serve immediately — Tortang Talong is best eaten fresh and hot!
