Pinoy Pork BBQ

Sweet-salty marinated pork skewers grilled over charcoal with banana ketchup baste — the ultimate Filipino street food.

🍖 Main Dish⏱ 15 min prep🕐 12 min grill👥 6–8 servings⭐ Easy

📝 Description

Pinoy Pork BBQ is a beloved Filipino street food — marinated pork skewers grilled over hot coals until charred and caramelized. The marinade is a sweet-salty-garlicky blend of soy sauce, banana ketchup, and Sprite that makes every bite irresistible.

🛒 Ingredients

Marinade:

  • 1 kg pork shoulder (kasim), sliced into thin strips
  • ½ cup soy sauce · ½ cup banana ketchup · ¼ cup Sprite or 7-Up
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar · 1 head garlic, minced · 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp calamansi juice · 2 tbsp oyster sauce

For Grilling:

  • Bamboo skewers (soaked 30 minutes) · Extra banana ketchup for basting

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix all marinade ingredients. Add pork strips and toss to coat. Marinate in refrigerator at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
  2. Thread marinated pork strips onto bamboo skewers, folding each strip accordion-style.
  3. Cook over hot charcoal or grill pan on medium-high heat, turning every 2–3 minutes.
  4. Brush with banana ketchup every time you flip. Grill until slightly charred, about 10–12 minutes total.
  5. Serve hot with steamed rice, atchara, and sawsawan (spiced vinegar dip).

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Freeze the pork for 30 minutes before slicing — easier to cut thin strips.
  • Use banana ketchup (not tomato ketchup) for the authentic Pinoy BBQ flavor.
  • Low, slow heat over charcoal gives the best smoky result.
