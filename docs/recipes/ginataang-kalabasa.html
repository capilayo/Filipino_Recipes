Ginataang Kalabasa

Squash and string beans in creamy coconut milk with shrimp and bagoong — nutritious and deeply satisfying.

?? Vegetable? 10 min prep?? 25 min cook?? 4–5 servings? Easy

?? Description

Ginataang Kalabasa is a creamy, comforting Filipino dish of squash and string beans slow-cooked in rich coconut milk. It's often paired with shrimp or pork, making it a nutritious and filling everyday meal. The natural sweetness of kalabasa perfectly balances the richness of gata.

?? Ingredients

  • 500g kalabasa (squash / kabocha), cubed
  • 1 bundle sitaw (string beans), cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 200g shrimp, peeled (or 200g pork, sliced thin)
  • 1 can (400ml) coconut milk (gata)
  • ½ cup coconut cream (kakang gata), for finishing
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced · 1 medium onion, sliced · 1 thumb ginger, julienned
  • 2 tbsp bagoong alamang (shrimp paste) — or fish sauce to taste
  • 1 pc long green chili (siling haba) · 1 tbsp cooking oil

????? Instructions

  1. Heat oil. Sauté garlic, onion, and ginger until fragrant.
  2. Add shrimp (or pork) and cook until pink/browned.
  3. Pour in coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add kalabasa cubes. Cook for 8–10 minutes until slightly tender.
  5. Add sitaw and siling haba. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in coconut cream and bagoong alamang. Simmer for 2–3 more minutes.
  7. Adjust seasoning. Serve hot with rice.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Don't overcook the sitaw — add it toward the end so it stays vibrant and slightly crisp.
  • For a richer dish, use fresh kakang gata (squeezed coconut cream) instead of canned.
  • Bagoong alamang adds a punchy umami depth — start with 1 tbsp and adjust to taste.
