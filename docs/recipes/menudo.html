Pork Menudo

Hearty tomato-based pork stew with potatoes, carrots, liver, and hotdog — a beloved fiesta and everyday ulam.

?? Main Dish ? 15 min prep ?? 50 min cook ?? 4–6 servings ? Easy

?? Description

Pork Menudo is a hearty Filipino tomato-based stew loaded with tender pork, potatoes, carrots, hotdog, and liver. It's a beloved fiesta dish that's equally great for everyday family meals — sweet, savory, and deeply satisfying.

?? Ingredients

  • 500g pork kasim (shoulder), cut into small cubes
  • 200g pork liver, cut into small cubes
  • 2 pcs hotdog, sliced
  • 2 pcs medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 pc medium carrot, cubed
  • 1 can (250ml) tomato sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 pc red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ cup water or broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • ½ cup green peas (optional)
  • ¼ cup raisins (optional, for a sweeter version)

????? Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan. Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant and softened.
  2. Add pork and cook until lightly browned on all sides.
  3. Pour in soy sauce and tomato sauce. Stir to combine.
  4. Add water or broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes and carrots. Cook for another 10 minutes until tender.
  6. Add pork liver, hotdog, and bell pepper. Simmer for 5–7 more minutes.
  7. Stir in green peas and raisins (if using). Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Serve hot with steaming white rice.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Add the liver last — overcooked liver turns tough and grainy.
  • A small handful of raisins balances the acidity of the tomato sauce beautifully.
  • Leftovers taste even better the next day as the flavors meld.
