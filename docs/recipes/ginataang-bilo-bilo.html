Ginataang Bilo-Bilo

Chewy rice balls with saba banana, sweet potato, and sago in sweet coconut milk — the ultimate rainy-day merienda.

?? Dessert? 20 min prep?? 30 min cook?? 6–8 servings? Easy

?? Description

Ginataang Bilo-Bilo is a beloved Filipino merienda and dessert — chewy glutinous rice balls simmered in sweet coconut milk together with saba bananas, jackfruit, sweet potato, taro, and tapioca pearls. Warm, creamy, and naturally sweet.

?? Ingredients

Bilo-Bilo (Rice Balls):

  • 2 cups glutinous rice flour (galapong) · ½–¾ cup water

Coconut Soup:

  • 2 cans (800ml) coconut milk · 1 cup water · ½ cup sugar
  • 3 pcs saba banana, sliced · 1 cup sweet potato (kamote), cubed
  • 1 cup taro (gabi), cubed · ½ cup young jackfruit (langka), shredded
  • ½ cup small tapioca pearls (sago), cooked separately until translucent
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional) · Pinch of salt

????? Instructions

  1. Mix glutinous rice flour with water until a smooth, pliable dough forms. Roll into small balls (~1-inch diameter). Set aside.
  2. In a large pot, combine coconut milk and water. Bring to a gentle boil. Add taro and sweet potato — simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Drop rice balls in carefully. They will float when cooked, about 5–7 minutes.
  4. Add saba banana, jackfruit, cooked sago, and sugar. Stir gently. Add salt and vanilla. Simmer another 5 minutes.
  5. Taste and add more sugar if needed. Serve warm or chilled.

?? Tips & Tricks

  • Pre-cook the sago separately — it takes 20–30 minutes to fully cook and become translucent.
  • Do not over-stir after adding the bilo-bilo or they will break apart.
  • For a richer, thicker ginataang, use pure coconut cream instead of coconut milk.
