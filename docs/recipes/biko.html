Biko

Chewy glutinous rice cake cooked in coconut milk and brown sugar with golden caramel latik topping — classic Filipino kakanin.

🍮 Dessert⏱ 10 min prep🕐 50 min cook👥 12–15 pcs⭐ Easy

📝 Description

Biko is a classic Filipino sticky rice cake (kakanin) made with glutinous rice cooked in coconut milk and brown sugar, topped with golden caramel latik or coconut curd. It's a staple at every Filipino celebration, town fiesta, and meryenda table — sweet, chewy, and utterly irresistible.

🛒 Ingredients

Biko:

  • 2 cups glutinous rice (malagkit), washed and soaked 1 hour
  • 2 cups coconut milk · 1½ cups water · 1½ cups dark brown sugar · ½ tsp salt

Latik Topping:

  • 1 cup coconut cream (kakang gata) · ½ cup dark brown sugar

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. In a pot, combine glutinous rice, 1 cup coconut milk, and water. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until rice is half-cooked (~15 minutes).
  2. In a separate pan, combine remaining 1 cup coconut milk with brown sugar and salt. Bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the half-cooked rice to the coconut-sugar sauce. Stir continuously over low heat until very thick (~15–20 minutes).
  4. For latik: combine coconut cream and brown sugar. Cook over medium heat, stirring until it caramelizes into thick, golden-brown syrup (~15 minutes).
  5. Transfer biko into a greased pan. Flatten evenly. Pour latik topping over and spread.
  6. Let cool completely before slicing and serving.

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Soak the glutinous rice beforehand for faster, more even cooking.
  • Use muscovado sugar for a deeper, more complex caramel flavor.
  • Biko keeps well at room temperature for 1–2 days — cover with plastic wrap.
