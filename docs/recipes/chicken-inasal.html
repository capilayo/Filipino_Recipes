Chicken Inasal

Bacolod-style grilled chicken marinated in calamansi and lemongrass, basted with annatto oil for that iconic orange-red glow.

🍖 Main Dish⏱ 20 min prep🕐 25 min grill👥 4 servings⭐ Medium

📝 Description

Chicken Inasal is the pride of Bacolod City — grilled chicken marinated in a vibrant blend of calamansi, vinegar, lemongrass, ginger, and annatto oil, then basted continuously during grilling to achieve that signature orange-red glow and deeply aromatic, tangy flavor.

🛒 Ingredients

Marinade:

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into quarters (or 4 pcs chicken thighs/drumsticks)
  • ½ cup calamansi juice · ¼ cup white cane vinegar
  • 3 stalks lemongrass, pounded and chopped · 1 thumb ginger, grated
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tsp salt · 1 tsp black pepper · 2 tbsp brown sugar

Annatto Basting Oil:

  • ½ cup cooking oil · 2 tbsp atsuete (annatto seeds) · 2 tbsp margarine or butter

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Make basting oil: heat oil in a small saucepan, add annatto seeds, simmer 3–5 minutes until oil turns deep orange-red. Strain seeds and add butter. Set aside.
  2. Combine all marinade ingredients. Score chicken pieces and submerge. Refrigerate at least 4 hours (overnight preferred).
  3. Prepare charcoal grill on medium heat. Place chicken pieces bone-side down first. Cook 8–10 minutes.
  4. Flip chicken and brush generously with annatto basting oil. Baste every 2–3 minutes while grilling.
  5. Total grill time: 20–25 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes before serving.
  6. Serve with garlic sinangag rice and spiced vinegar dip.

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • The more you baste with annatto oil, the deeper and more beautiful the color.
  • Adding ginger and lemongrass directly to the charcoal creates amazing aromatic smoke.
  • Authentic inasal is eaten with chicken oil drizzled over garlic rice — save the basting oil for this!
