# 🍚 Arroz Caldo

**Category:** Soup / Congee | **Prep Time:** 10 mins | **Cook Time:** 45 mins | **Servings:** 4–6 | **Difficulty:** Easy

---

## 📝 Description

Arroz Caldo is the Filipino answer to chicken congee — a warm, comforting rice porridge cooked with chicken, ginger, and garlic, topped with crispy fried garlic, spring onions, and a drizzle of calamansi. It's the ultimate Filipino comfort food, perfect for rainy days, mañanita mornings, or when someone needs healing.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 500g chicken (thighs or drumsticks), bone-in
- 1 cup glutinous rice (malagkit) or regular rice
- 6 cups chicken broth or water
- 1 thumb ginger, julienned
- 5 cloves garlic, minced (half for cooking, half for topping)
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- Salt to taste

**Toppings:**
- Crispy fried garlic
- Spring onions, sliced
- Hard-boiled eggs, halved
- Calamansi (or lemon)
- Fish sauce on the side
- Toasted pinipig (optional)

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot. Sauté garlic and ginger until golden and fragrant.
2. Add onion and cook until translucent.
3. Add chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned.
4. Add fish sauce and stir for 1–2 minutes.
5. Pour in broth or water. Bring to a boil.
6. Add rice. Stir regularly to prevent sticking. Simmer on medium-low heat.
7. Cook for 30–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rice is very soft and porridge-like.
8. Season with salt and pepper.
9. Serve hot, topped with fried garlic, spring onions, egg, and a squeeze of calamansi.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- Use **glutinous rice (malagkit)** for a thicker, creamier texture.
- Stir frequently — rice settles and burns at the bottom if left unattended.
- Topped with **tokwa't baboy** (fried tofu and pork), it becomes a full meal.
