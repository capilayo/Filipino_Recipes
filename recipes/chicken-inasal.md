# Chicken Inasal (Bacolod-Style Grilled Chicken)

## Description
Chicken Inasal is the pride of Bacolod City — grilled chicken marinated in a vibrant blend of calamansi, vinegar, lemongrass, ginger, and annatto oil, then basted continuously during grilling to achieve that signature orange-red glow, charred skin, and deeply aromatic, tangy flavor. Nothing compares to the real thing, eaten by hand with garlic rice soaked in inasal oil.

---

## Ingredients
*(Serves 4)*

**Marinade:**
- 1 whole chicken, cut into quarters (or 4 pcs chicken thighs/drumsticks)
- ½ cup calamansi juice (about 15–18 calamansi)
- ¼ cup white cane vinegar (sukang maasim)
- 3 stalks lemongrass, pounded and chopped
- 1 thumb ginger, grated
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp brown sugar

**Annatto Basting Oil:**
- ½ cup cooking oil
- 2 tbsp atsuete (annatto seeds)
- 2 tbsp margarine or butter

---

## Instructions

1. **Make basting oil** — Heat oil in a small saucepan. Add annatto seeds and simmer on low for 3–5 minutes until oil turns deep orange-red. Strain seeds and add butter. Set aside.
2. **Marinate chicken** — Combine all marinade ingredients and mix well. Score chicken pieces and submerge in marinade. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (overnight preferred).
3. **Grill** — Prepare charcoal grill on medium heat. Place chicken pieces bone-side down first. Cook 8–10 minutes.
4. **Baste continuously** — Flip chicken and brush generously with annatto basting oil. Baste every 2–3 minutes while grilling. Total grill time: 20–25 minutes.
5. **Rest** — Let rest 5 minutes before serving. Serve with garlic sinangag rice and spiced vinegar dip.

---

## Tips
- The more you baste with annatto oil, the deeper and more beautiful the color.
- Adding a small piece of ginger and lemongrass directly to the charcoal creates amazing aromatic smoke.
- Authentic inasal is eaten with chicken oil drizzled over garlic rice — save the basting oil for this!
