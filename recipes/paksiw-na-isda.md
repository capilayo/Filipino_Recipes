# 🐟 Paksiw na Isda

**Category:** Main Dish | **Prep Time:** 10 mins | **Cook Time:** 20 mins | **Servings:** 3–4 | **Difficulty:** Easy

---

## 📝 Description

Paksiw na Isda is a simple and classic Filipino dish where fish is simmered in vinegar, garlic, ginger, and spices until tender and tangy. It's one of the quickest Filipino dishes to prepare — minimal ingredients, maximum flavor, and the perfect everyday ulam.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 500g bangus (milkfish), tilapia, or any whole fish, cleaned and sliced
- 1/2 cup white vinegar (or sukang paombong)
- 1/2 cup water
- 5 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized ginger, sliced
- 1 pc long green chili (siling haba)
- 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis) or salt to taste
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 2 pcs eggplant, sliced (optional)

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. Arrange garlic, ginger, and eggplant (if using) at the bottom of a shallow pot.
2. Place the fish pieces on top.
3. Pour in vinegar, water, and oil. Add peppercorns and chili.
4. Season with fish sauce or salt.
5. Bring to a boil over medium heat. **Do not stir** — let the vinegar cook off the sharp acidity.
6. Reduce heat and simmer for 10–15 minutes until fish is cooked through and sauce reduces slightly.
7. Serve immediately with hot white rice.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- **Never stir** paksiw while it's cooking — it breaks the fish and makes the sauce cloudy.
- Sukang **Paombong** (palm vinegar) gives a more traditional, mellow flavor than white vinegar.
- Adding **eggplant** (talong) soaks up the vinegary sauce beautifully.
