# 🍆 Tortang Talong

**Category:** Main Dish / Egg Dish | **Prep Time:** 15 mins | **Cook Time:** 20 mins | **Servings:** 3–4 | **Difficulty:** Easy

---

## 📝 Description

Tortang Talong is a Filipino eggplant omelette made by roasting talong (eggplant) over an open flame, peeling off the charred skin, dipping it in beaten egg, and pan-frying it to golden perfection. It's simple, smoky, and delicious — one of the most satisfying quick Filipino meals you can make with just a few ingredients.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 3–4 pcs medium talong (eggplant), whole
- 3 pcs eggs, beaten
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 3 tbsp cooking oil

**Optional additions (giniling version):**
- 200g ground pork or beef
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 small onion, minced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. **Roast the eggplant:** Place whole eggplants directly over a gas flame or on a grill. Rotate occasionally until the skin is completely charred and the flesh is soft, about 8–10 minutes.
2. Let cool slightly. Peel off the charred skin, keeping the stem intact for handling.
3. Gently flatten each eggplant with a fork to create a fan shape.
4. Season the beaten eggs with salt and pepper.
5. Dip the flattened eggplant completely in the egg mixture.
6. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Slide the egg-coated eggplant into the pan.
7. Pour a little extra egg over the top. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side until golden.
8. Serve immediately with rice and banana ketchup or spiced vinegar.

**For the giniling (ground meat) version:**
- Sauté garlic, onion, and ground meat with soy sauce until cooked. Season.
- Spread the cooked meat over the flattened eggplant before dipping in egg.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- **Roasting directly over a flame** gives that authentic smoky flavor — don't use an oven if you can help it.
- The **stem** helps you handle and flip the eggplant without breaking it.
- Serve immediately — Tortang Talong is best eaten fresh and hot!
