# 🍜 Palabok (Pancit Palabok)

**Category:** Noodles | **Prep Time:** 20 mins | **Cook Time:** 30 mins | **Servings:** 6–8 | **Difficulty:** Medium

---

## 📝 Description

Pancit Palabok is a beloved Filipino noodle dish smothered in a vibrant shrimp-based orange sauce, topped with hard-boiled eggs, chicharon, tinapa flakes, spring onions, and calamansi. It's a fiesta favorite and one of the most festive and colorful dishes in Filipino cuisine.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

**Noodles:**
- 400g bihon (rice noodles) or canton noodles, soaked in water

**Palabok Sauce:**
- 2 cups shrimp broth (from heads and shells)
- 200g ground pork
- 3 tbsp annatto powder (or achuete seeds dissolved in water) — for color
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour or cornstarch (dissolved in 1/4 cup water)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Toppings:**
- Hard-boiled eggs, sliced
- Chicharon (pork crackling), crushed
- Smoked tinapa flakes (or flaked fish)
- 200g shrimp, boiled and peeled
- Spring onions, sliced
- Calamansi, halved
- Fried garlic

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. **Make shrimp broth:** Boil shrimp heads and shells in 2–3 cups water for 10 minutes. Strain and reserve broth.
2. **Cook the sauce:** Heat oil. Sauté garlic and onion. Add ground pork and cook through.
3. Pour in shrimp broth + annatto water. Bring to a simmer.
4. Add the dissolved flour/cornstarch. Stir until sauce thickens to a pourable consistency.
5. Season with fish sauce, salt, and pepper.
6. **Cook noodles:** Boil noodles for 2–3 minutes until just tender. Drain.
7. Arrange noodles on a large platter. Pour generous sauce over the top.
8. Top with shrimp, egg, chicharon, tinapa, spring onions, and fried garlic.
9. Serve with calamansi halves on the side.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- **Annatto (achuete)** gives the signature orange color — don't skip it!
- Soak bihon noodles **just until pliable** before cooking to avoid mushiness.
- Add chicharon **right before serving** so it stays crunchy.
