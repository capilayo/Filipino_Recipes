# Pinoy Pork BBQ (Filipino Barbecue Skewers)

## Description
Pinoy Pork BBQ is a beloved Filipino street food — marinated pork skewers grilled over hot coals until charred and caramelized. The marinade is a sweet-salty-garlicky blend of soy sauce, banana ketchup, and Sprite that makes every bite irresistible. Whether it's a fiesta or a weekend grilling session, this is always the first to disappear from the table.

---

## Ingredients
*(Serves 6–8)*

**Pork Marinade:**
- 1 kg pork shoulder (kasim), sliced into thin strips
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ½ cup banana ketchup
- ¼ cup Sprite or 7-Up
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 head garlic, minced
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp calamansi juice (or lemon juice)
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce

**For Grilling:**
- Bamboo skewers (soaked in water 30 minutes)
- Extra banana ketchup for basting

---

## Instructions

1. **Marinate** — Mix all marinade ingredients in a large bowl. Add pork strips and toss to coat. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
2. **Skewer** — Thread marinated pork strips onto bamboo skewers, folding each strip accordion-style.
3. **Grill** — Cook over hot charcoal or a grill pan on medium-high heat, turning every 2–3 minutes.
4. **Baste** — Brush with banana ketchup every time you flip. Grill until slightly charred, about 10–12 minutes total.
5. **Serve** — Serve hot with steamed rice, atchara (pickled papaya), and sawsawan (spiced vinegar dip).

---

## Tips
- Freeze the pork for 30 minutes before slicing — it's easier to cut thin strips.
- Use banana ketchup (not tomato ketchup) for the authentic Pinoy BBQ flavor.
- Low, slow heat over charcoal gives the best smoky result.
