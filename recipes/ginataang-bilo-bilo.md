# Ginataang Bilo-Bilo (Sweet Rice Balls in Coconut Milk)

## Description
Ginataang Bilo-Bilo is a beloved Filipino merienda (snack) and dessert — chewy glutinous rice balls simmered in sweet coconut milk together with saba bananas, jackfruit, sweet potato, taro, and tapioca pearls. Warm, creamy, and naturally sweet, this kakanin is a rainy-day treat and a festive fiesta staple that brings everyone to the table.

---

## Ingredients
*(Serves 6–8)*

**Bilo-Bilo (Rice Balls):**
- 2 cups glutinous rice flour (galapong)
- ½–¾ cup water (enough to make a soft dough)

**Coconut Soup:**
- 2 cans (800ml) coconut milk
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup sugar (or to taste)
- 3 pcs saba banana (cooking banana), sliced
- 1 cup sweet potato (kamote), peeled and cubed
- 1 cup taro (gabi), peeled and cubed
- ½ cup young jackfruit (langka), shredded (canned is fine)
- ½ cup small tapioca pearls (sago), cooked separately until translucent
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- Pinch of salt

---

## Instructions

1. **Make bilo-bilo** — Mix glutinous rice flour with water until a smooth, pliable dough forms (like playdough). Roll into small balls (about 1-inch diameter). Set aside.
2. **Cook hard ingredients** — In a large pot, combine coconut milk and water. Bring to a gentle boil. Add taro and sweet potato — simmer for 10 minutes until beginning to soften.
3. **Add bilo-bilo** — Drop rice balls in carefully. They will float when cooked, about 5–7 minutes.
4. **Add remaining** — Add saba banana, jackfruit, cooked sago, and sugar. Stir gently. Add salt and vanilla. Simmer another 5 minutes.
5. **Adjust** — Taste and add more sugar if needed. Serve warm. Can also be served chilled.

---

## Tips
- Pre-cook the sago separately — it takes 20–30 minutes to fully cook and become translucent.
- Do not over-stir after adding the bilo-bilo or they will break apart.
- For a richer, thicker ginataang, use pure coconut cream instead of coconut milk.

🥥 GINATAANG BILO-BILO — Mainit, Matamis, at Sobrang Sarap! 🍌

Pag maulan, ginataang bilo-bilo ang sagot! Malapot na gata, malambot na bilo-bilo, at tamis ng saging at kamote — ito na ang pinakamagandang merienda! 😍

**Kailangan mo:**
🍚 2 cups galapong (glutinous rice flour)
🥥 2 cans coconut milk
🍌 3 pcs saging saba, hiniwa
🍠 Kamote at gabi, hiniwa
🫐 Sago, niluto na
🍬 ½ cup asukal

**Paano gawin:**
1. Gumawa ng bilo-bilo — bilugin ang masa ng galapong
2. Pakuluin ang gata + tubig — lutuin ang gabi at kamote ng 10 minuto
3. Ihulog ang mga bilo-bilo — float na sila pag luto na (5–7 minuto)
4. Dagdagan ng saging, langka, sago, at asukal
5. Haluin nang dahan-dahan — lutuin ng 5 minuto pa — tapos!

💡 **Tip:** Iluto ang sago nang hiwalay — 20–30 minuto bago transparent sila!
