# 🥬 Sinigang na Baboy (Pork Sinigang)

**Category:** Soup | **Prep Time:** 15 mins | **Cook Time:** 50 mins | **Servings:** 5–6 | **Difficulty:** Easy

---

## 📝 Description

Sinigang na Baboy is the hearty, tamarind-soured version of Sinigang using pork ribs or pork belly. Loaded with kangkong, sitaw, gabi, and radish in a tangy, savory broth, it's one of the most universally loved Filipino soups — pure comfort in a bowl. While the original Sinigang na Baboy recipe is already in the collection, this version uses fresh tamarind and highlights different vegetable combinations.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 1 kg pork ribs or pork belly, cut into pieces
- 8 cups water
- 5–6 pcs fresh sampalok (tamarind) — or 1 packet sinigang mix
- 2 pcs medium tomatoes, quartered
- 1 medium onion, quartered
- 3 pcs gabi (taro), peeled and halved
- 1 bundle kangkong (water spinach), leaves only
- 5 pcs sitaw (string beans), cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 pc labanos (white radish), sliced
- 2 pcs siling haba (long green chili)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
- Salt to taste

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. In a large pot, boil pork in water with tomatoes and onion. Skim off foam.
2. Simmer for 30–35 minutes until pork is tender.
3. If using fresh tamarind: boil separately in 1 cup water, mash, and strain the juice into the pot.
4. Add gabi and labanos. Cook for 10 minutes.
5. Add sitaw and siling haba. Cook for 3 minutes.
6. Season with fish sauce and salt.
7. Add kangkong last. Cook for 1–2 minutes only — just until wilted.
8. Serve immediately with hot white rice and patis on the side.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- Always add **kangkong last** — it overcooks in seconds and turns slimy.
- **Gabi (taro)** naturally thickens the broth and adds a creamy richness.
- For **extra sour** sinigang, add a splash of tamarind concentrate or an extra packet of mix.
